Caroline Hamba

Advocate of the High Court of Kenya · Principal Advocate · Commissioner for Oaths & Notary Public

A practical approach to advocacy and client service.

Caroline Hamba leads Hamba Caroline & Co. Advocates from Eldoret. Her work is centred on understanding each client's legal position, preparing the relevant record and selecting an appropriate route to resolve or advance the matter.

Her practice includes litigation and appeals, family and matrimonial-property matters, property, employment, commercial advisory and related private-client legal work.
